Late-night conversation, same session as the plan-writing. Pyro's prediction
and intention, verbatim core: agents will soon produce too much for humans
to review, so we need a validation system that can prove "to a certain
degree the same as a human reviewer could." His design: split software into
contracts of **measurable obligations** (100% decidable — true/false or a
value), each provable by a test, plus a small **external intelligence that
is neither the coding agent nor the human — the contract's own guardian —
whose job is to make sure obligation and test are not drifting.**

## How this maps

- Docket v0 = the courtroom record at human scale (the clerk). The guardian
  layer = scaling the verdict moment. Two products, one substrate.
- Pre-registered in the design: concepts/03 falsifier #4's lesson
  (risk-tiered auto-acceptance, deliberately deferred) + KG thread 9
  (verifier independence, review-burden metrics, adaptive-attack evaluation
  of acceptance courts).
- Directly answers the hollow-oracle watch item in TH-0001 (signed law,
  unsigned oracle seam): the guardian is the dedicated intelligence on that
  seam.

## Separation of powers (three parties)

Worker fulfills (untrusted, optimizes green) · Guardian audits the LAW's
integrity (bound to contract, never authors work or harness) · Human signs
law and takes escalations only.

## Guardian mechanics (receipts, not vibes)

1. **Obligation-guided mutation testing** — inject targeted violations of
   the obligation; if the test stays green under a violating mutant, the
   oracle is hollow, proven. Attestation = "N/N targeted mutants killed."
2. **Reverse blind replay** — can a fresh agent reconstruct the obligation
   from the test alone? Under-determination gets named, not felt.
3. **Evidence audit** — re-execute bundle references, refuse vibes (the
   door-for-evidence that falsifier #3's lesson predicted).

## Who guards the guardian

Calibration by use (third instance of the trick: clause-defects measure the
law, evidence-defects measure the filer, attestation-failures measure the
guardian) + recorded independence metadata (model/prompt/context disjoint
from worker — thread 9's verifier-independence schema).

## Boundary

Guardian is an officer of the court built ON the agent surface, not IN the
clerk. Docket's only eventual schema growth: an attestation record kind +
risk-tier verdict policy (= the deferred multi-party authority). The
falsifier run generates the evidence for when.

## Portfolio convergence (signal)

Third independent appearance of the same role: Canon's **keeper**
(custody/oath/succession), Codex-SDK **resident maintainer** (active
artifacts), docket's **guardian**. A persistent small intelligence bound to
an artifact rather than a task, keeping the artifact's promises true over
time. Likely a missing primitive; candidate for its own KG capture when it
firms up.
